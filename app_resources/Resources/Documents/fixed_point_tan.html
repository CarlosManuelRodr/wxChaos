Fixed Point: tan(z)

A convergence map for repeated tangent iteration.

Formula

\(z_{n+1}=\tan(z_n)\). A point is considered converged when successive iterates differ by less than the configured minimum step.

Background

The poles of the tangent function make this fixed-point map especially sharp and discontinuous compared with sine or cosine.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Option: minimum convergence step.
  • Algorithm: convergence test.
