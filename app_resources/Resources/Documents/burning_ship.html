Burning Ship

A Mandelbrot-like set that folds each orbit into the first quadrant before squaring.

Formula

\(z_{n+1}=(|\operatorname{Re} z_n|+i|\operatorname{Im} z_n|)^2+c\).

Background

The Burning Ship fractal is usually credited to Michael Michelitsch and Otto Roessler. Its name comes from the silhouette-like shapes that appear near its main body.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
