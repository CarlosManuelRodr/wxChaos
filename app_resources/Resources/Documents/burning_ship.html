Burning Ship

A Mandelbrot relative with its sails folded into intricate details.

\(z_{n+1}=(|\operatorname{Re}(z_n)|+i|\operatorname{Im}(z_n)|)^2+c\)

The Burning Ship starts with the familiar escape-time ritual, then changes one gesture: before squaring, it reflects the orbit into the first quadrant. That small fold turns round bulbs into masts, decks, and very intricate patterns.

Mathematical notes

The formula behind the fold

The Burning Ship uses \(z_0=0\) and \(z_{n+1}=(|\operatorname{Re}(z_n)|+i|\operatorname{Im}(z_n)|)^2+c\). The absolute values reflect every orbit step before the square is applied. Because complex conjugation and absolute values break complex differentiability, the geometry behaves differently from analytic quadratic maps such as Mandelbrot.

Notas matemáticas

La fórmula detrás del pliegue

El Burning Ship usa \(z_0=0\) y \(z_{n+1}=(|\operatorname{Re}(z_n)|+i|\operatorname{Im}(z_n)|)^2+c\). Los valores absolutos reflejan cada paso de la órbita antes de aplicar el cuadrado. Como la conjugación compleja y los valores absolutos rompen la diferenciabilidad compleja, la geometría se comporta de forma distinta a la de los mapas cuadráticos analíticos, como el de Mandelbrot.
