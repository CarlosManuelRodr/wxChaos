Burning Ship

A Mandelbrot-like set that folds each orbit into the first quadrant before squaring.

Formula

\(z_{0}=0,\quad z_{n+1}=(|\operatorname{Re} z_n|+i|\operatorname{Im} z_n|)^2+c\). The absolute values introduce the sharp, flame-like folds.

Background

The Burning Ship fractal is usually credited to Michael Michelitsch and Otto Roessler. Its name comes from the silhouettes near the main body and from the way its lower filaments resemble smoke, masts, and reflected hulls.
