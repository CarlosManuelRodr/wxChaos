Tricorn

The Mandelbar set, made by conjugating the orbit before squaring.

Formula

\(z_{0}=0,\quad z_{n+1}=\overline{z_n}^{\,2}+c\).

Background

The tricorn is the best-known antiholomorphic relative of the Mandelbrot set. Its three-cornered symmetry gives it the common name.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
