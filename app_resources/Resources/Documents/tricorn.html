Tricorn

The Mandelbar set, made by conjugating the orbit before squaring.

Formula

\(z_{0}=0,\quad z_{n+1}=\overline{z_n}^{\,2}+c\). Complex conjugation makes this an antiholomorphic cousin of the Mandelbrot set.

Background

The tricorn is the best-known member of the Mandelbar family. It keeps the parameter-plane spirit of Mandelbrot, but conjugation reverses orientation on each step, producing three-cornered symmetry and different boundary behavior.
