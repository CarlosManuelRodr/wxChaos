Fixed Point: cos(z)

A convergence map for repeated cosine iteration.

Formula

\(z_{n+1}=\cos(z_n)\). A point is considered converged when successive iterates differ by less than the configured minimum step.

Background

Complex trigonometric fixed-point iteration reveals basins and boundaries that are hidden in the real one-dimensional picture.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High-precision rendering.
  • Option: minimum convergence step.
  • Algorithm: convergence test.
