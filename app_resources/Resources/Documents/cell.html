Cell

A coupled rational escape-time formula with compartment-like regions.

Formula

For each pixel \(c\), wxChaos starts \(z_0=c\), \(b_0=c-\sin(c)\). Then \(b_{n+1}=\frac{b_n}{c}\), \(z_{n+1}=z_n c+\frac{b_{n+1}}{z_n}\). The bailout option controls the escape radius.

Options

Bailout
The escape radius. Larger values allow more orbit growth before the point is marked outside.

Background

Cell is another wxChaos-specific formula. Its name comes from the compartment-like shapes that appear when the rational terms partition the plane into repeated pockets.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering and orbit display.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
