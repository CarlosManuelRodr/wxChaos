Cell

An escape-time formula with cellular-looking regions.

Formula

A custom complex recurrence implemented by the Cell renderer.

Background

This app-specific formula is named for the compartment-like regions it can produce. It is useful as a reminder that visual taxonomy often follows the picture first and the formal family second.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
