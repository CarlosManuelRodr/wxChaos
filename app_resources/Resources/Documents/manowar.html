Manowar

An escape-time fractal with one-step orbit memory.

Formula

A Mandelbrot-like recurrence where the next value depends on both \(z_n\) and a remembered previous value.

Background

The Manowar family is a less canonical experimental escape-time family. Memory terms change the local feedback of the orbit and can produce heavy, bulbous structures.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
