Manowar

A Mandelbrot-like escape-time fractal with one-step orbit memory.

Formula

For each pixel \(c\), wxChaos starts \(z_0=c\) and \(m_0=z_0\). Then \(z_{n+1}=z_n^2+m_n+c\), \(m_{n+1}=z_n\). The point escapes when \(|z_n|>2\).

Background

The Manowar family is an experimental escape-time family. Adding a memory term changes the feedback loop from a simple quadratic iteration into a second-order recurrence. That extra state can thicken bulbs, stretch filaments, and make the parameter plane feel less symmetric than the Mandelbrot set.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering and orbit display.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
