Fixed Point: sin(z)

A convergence map for repeated sine iteration.

Formula

\(z_{n+1}=\sin(z_n)\). A point is considered converged when successive iterates differ by less than the configured minimum step.

Background

Fixed-point fractals color starting points by how repeated application of a function converges, cycles, or fails to settle.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Option: minimum convergence step.
  • Algorithm: convergence test.
