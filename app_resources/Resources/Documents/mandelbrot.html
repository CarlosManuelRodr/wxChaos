Mandelbrot Set

The classical connectedness map for quadratic complex dynamics.

Formula

\(z_{0}=0,\quad z_{n+1}=z_n^2+c\). A point \(c\) is drawn inside when the orbit remains bounded.

Background

The set is named after Benoit Mandelbrot, whose computer visualizations helped make it one of the icons of fractal geometry. It is closely related to earlier work by Gaston Julia and Pierre Fatou on iterating complex functions.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision and fast perturbation rendering.
  • Orbit display, smooth coloring, orbit traps, and Buddhabrot.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality, Buddhabrot.
