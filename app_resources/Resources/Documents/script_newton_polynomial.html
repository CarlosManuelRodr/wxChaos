Newton \(z^8+15z^4-16=0\)

A higher-degree root map with layered symmetry.

\(z_{n+1}=z_n-\frac{z_n^8+15z_n^4-16}{8z_n^7+60z_n^3}\)

Higher-degree polynomials give Newton's method more destinations. This script turns those destinations into a complex basin map where symmetry and sensitivity compete.

Mathematical notes

The polynomial step

The function is \(f(z)=z^8+15z^4-16\), with derivative \(f'(z)=8z^7+60z^3\). Newton's method applies \(z_{n+1}=z_n-f(z_n)/f'(z_n)\). The script colors by convergence speed with a simple final-position classification.
