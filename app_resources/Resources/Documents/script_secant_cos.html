Secant \(\cos(z)-1=0\)

Root finding without an explicit derivative.

\(z_{n+1}=z_n-f(z_n)\frac{z_n-z_{n-1}}{f(z_n)-f(z_{n-1})},\quad f(z)=\cos(z)-1\)

The secant method estimates slope from two recent points instead of computing a derivative. On the complex plane, that extra memory gives the basin map a different rhythm from Newton's method.

Mathematical notes

The secant idea

The secant method replaces \(f'(z_n)\) with a finite difference from two points: \(z_{n+1}=z_n-f(z_n)(z_n-z_{n-1})/(f(z_n)-f(z_{n-1}))\). This script applies that derivative-free idea to a cosine-based equation.

Notas matemáticas

La idea del método de la secante

El método de la secante sustituye \(f'(z_n)\) por una diferencia finita calculada a partir de dos puntos: \(z_{n+1}=z_n-f(z_n)(z_n-z_{n-1})/(f(z_n)-f(z_{n-1}))\). Este script aplica esa idea sin derivadas a una ecuación basada en el coseno.
