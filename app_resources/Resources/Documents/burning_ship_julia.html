Burning Ship Julia

A fixed-parameter dynamical-plane view of the Burning Ship formula.

Formula

\(z_{n+1}=(|\operatorname{Re} z_n|+i|\operatorname{Im} z_n|)^2+k\).

Background

This variant fixes the complex parameter and lets each pixel supply the starting point, making it analogous to a Julia set.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
