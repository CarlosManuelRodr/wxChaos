Secant \(z^3-1=0\)

A three-root basin map driven by a two-point method.

\(z_{n+1}=z_n-f(z_n)\frac{z_n-z_{n-1}}{f(z_n)-f(z_{n-1})},\quad f(z)=z^3-1\)

This script revisits the classic cubic roots of unity, but uses the secant method instead of Newton's method. The result is a familiar destination map with a different path-finding personality.

Mathematical notes

Why secant maps differ from Newton maps

Newton's method uses a derivative at the current point. The secant method estimates that derivative from the current and previous values. Because the iteration state remembers two points, its basin boundaries can differ noticeably even for the same equation.
