Newton \(\cos(z)-z=0\)

A root-finding map for the complex fixed points of cosine.

\(z_{n+1}=z_n-\frac{\cos(z_n)-z_n}{-\sin(z_n)-1}\)

On the real line, \(\cos(x)=x\) has a famous fixed point. In the complex plane, Newton's method reveals a richer geography of guesses, basins, and challenging boundary corridors.

Mathematical notes

Newton's method for cosine fixed points

The equation is \(f(z)=\cos(z)-z\). Since \(f'(z)=-\sin(z)-1\), Newton's step is \(z_{n+1}=z_n+(\cos(z_n)-z_n)/(\sin(z_n)+1)\). Colors are offset by the final direction of the converged point.
