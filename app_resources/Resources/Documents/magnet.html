Magnet

A rational map from the magnet-fractal family.

Formula

wxChaos renders the type-1 magnet map \(z_0=0,\quad z_{n+1}=\left(\frac{z_n^2+c-1}{2z_n+c-2}\right)^2\), with each pixel supplying \(c\). The orbit is tested as an escape process.

Background

Magnet fractals are not magnetic-field simulations. They are rational iterations whose basins and filaments resemble magnetic poles and field lines. Paul Bourke's notes describe the classic magnet formulas and show how changing the rational map produces different members of the family.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
