Magnet

A fractal where attraction and escape compete through a fraction.

\(z_{n+1}=\left(\frac{z_n^2+c-1}{2z_n+c-2}\right)^2\)

The Magnet formula does not merely square and add. It divides one polynomial expression by another before squaring the result.

The formula originated in theoretical physics, where it was developed in the study of magnetic phase transitions.

Mathematical notes

The wxChaos Magnet formula

wxChaos starts at \(z_0=0\) and iterates \(z_{n+1}=\left(\frac{z_n^2+c-1}{2z_n+c-2}\right)^2\). Because this is a rational function, the denominator can strongly magnify nearby points. That stretching is part of what gives the Magnet set its distinctive geometry.

Magnet

Un fractal en el que la atracción y el escape compiten mediante una fracción.

\(z_{n+1}=\left(\frac{z_n^2+c-1}{2z_n+c-2}\right)^2\)

La fórmula Magnet no se limita a elevar al cuadrado y sumar. Divide una expresión polinómica entre otra antes de elevar el resultado al cuadrado.

La fórmula se originó en la física teórica, donde fue desarrollada para estudiar transiciones de fase magnéticas.
