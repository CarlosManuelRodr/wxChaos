Magnet

A rational-map fractal with magnetic-looking attraction basins.

Formula

A rational iteration related to the classical magnet fractal family.

Background

Magnet fractals are named for the way their basins appear to pull the plane into lobes and poles. They are commonly explored as rational maps rather than simple polynomial escape-time sets.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
