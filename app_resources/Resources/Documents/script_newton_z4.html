Newton \(z^4-1=0\)

Four roots, four destinations, and a fractal border between them.

This script turns Newton's method into a compass rose. Each pixel is a starting guess for \(z^4=1\); the color records how quickly the guess settles and which region of the complex plane it approaches.

What to try first

Look for the places where four colored basins nearly touch. Those are the sensitive points where a tiny change in the starting guess can choose another root.

Increase the iteration count and watch boundary regions sharpen rather than simply fill in.

Compare it with the built-in Newton fractal and the user-defined Newton room.

Mathematical notes

The Newton step

For \(f(z)=z^4-1\), Newton's method uses \(z_{n+1}=z_n-(z_n^4-1)/(4z_n^3)\). The roots are \(1\), \(-1\), \(i\), and \(-i\). The script stops when consecutive iterates are within a small tolerance.
