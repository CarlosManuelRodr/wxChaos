Fractory

An application-specific escape-time recurrence with two coupled complex states.

Formula

For each pixel \(c\), wxChaos starts \(z_0=c\), \(b_0=c-\sin(c)\). Then \(b_{n+1}=c+\frac{b_n}{c}-z_n\), \(z_{n+1}=z_n c+\frac{b_{n+1}}{z_n}\). Escape is detected when \(|z_n|>2\).

Background

Fractory is a wxChaos-grown formula: a small algebraic machine preserved because the image has a distinctive structure. The coupled \(z\) and \(b\) states make it feel related to second-order or memory-based escape-time systems, but with rational terms that can fold the plane sharply near zeros.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering and orbit display.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
