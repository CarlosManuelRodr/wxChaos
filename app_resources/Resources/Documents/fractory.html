Fractory

An exploratory escape-time formula from wxChaos.

Formula

A custom complex recurrence implemented by the Fractory renderer.

Background

Fractory is treated here as an application-specific formula. It belongs to the tradition of trying small changes to escape-time recurrences and preserving the ones with distinctive structure.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
