Sine Map

A transcendental Julia-style escape-time fractal.

Formula

\(z_{n+1}=k\sin(z_n)\).

Background

Replacing a polynomial with a transcendental function creates repeated structures caused by the periodic nature of sine. These maps are useful for seeing how complex trigonometric functions fold the plane.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Orbit display, smooth coloring, and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
