Jellyfish

A Julia-style escape-time fractal using a fractional power.

Formula

A Julia-style recurrence with a fractional exponent and a fixed complex parameter.

Background

This is an exploratory variant rather than a standard named mathematical object. Its name comes from the soft radial arms and tendrils that often appear in the rendered image.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
