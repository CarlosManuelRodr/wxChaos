Double Pendulum

A dynamical-system fractal based on chaotic pendulum motion.

Formula

Each pixel supplies initial angles. The renderer integrates the double-pendulum equations and tests bailout conditions.

Background

The double pendulum is a classic example of deterministic chaos: tiny changes in starting angles can produce very different trajectories.

wxChaos capabilities

  • Configurable masses, length, gravity, timestep, bailout conditions, and Runge-Kutta mode.
  • Orbit drawing for the simulated angular trajectory.
  • Algorithms: escape time and escape angle.
