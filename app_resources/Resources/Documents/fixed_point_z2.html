Fixed Point: z^2

A convergence map for repeated squaring.

Formula

\(z_{n+1}=z_n^2\). Points inside the unit disk converge to \(0\); points outside it escape; points on the unit circle form the delicate boundary.

Background

Repeated squaring is one of the simplest complex dynamical systems. It is a clean way to see attraction, escape, and a boundary where neither behavior is stable under perturbation.
