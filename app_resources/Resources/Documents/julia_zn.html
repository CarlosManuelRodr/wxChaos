Julia Zn

A Julia family where the exponent becomes a sculpting tool.

The familiar quadratic Julia set uses \(z^2+k\). Julia Zn lets you raise \(z\) to a chosen power, producing extra arms, symmetries, and rotational rhythms.

Open in wxChaosToggle orbit display

What to try first

Set \(n=3\) or \(n=4\). Look for the way additional powers add rotational structure.

Adjust the parameter \(k\) slightly. Watch the difference between a connected body and a broken dust of islands.

Use orbit display near one arm and compare it with the matching arm after rotation.

Mathematical notes

What the exponent does

wxChaos iterates \(z_{n+1}=z_n^p+k\), where the pixel supplies \(z_0\), \(k\) is fixed, and \(p\) is the chosen exponent. Increasing \(p\) increases the natural rotational symmetry of the map and changes how quickly large values escape.
