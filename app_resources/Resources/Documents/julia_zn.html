Julia ZN

A Julia set for higher-power polynomial iteration.

Formula

\(z_{n+1}=z_n^p+k\).

Background

Power Julia sets show how the degree of a polynomial changes the geometry of basins, filaments, and rotational symmetry around critical points.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Orbit display, smooth coloring, and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
