User Defined Escape Time

Write the rule and let the plane answer back.

After visiting the galleries, this room lets you become the curator. Choose a recurrence using \(z\) and \(c\), then let wxChaos turn boundedness and escape into an image.

What to try first

Begin with a known rule such as z = z^2 + c. Confirm that you recognize the behavior before changing it.

Change one ingredient at a time: the power, a sine term, a conjugate, or a small constant.

Use orbit display to debug your intuition. If the image surprises you, the orbit usually explains why.

Mathematical notes

What escape-time means

An escape-time renderer repeats a recurrence until either the orbit passes a bailout radius or the iteration limit is reached. Pixels that escape are colored by when or how they escaped; pixels that do not escape are treated as inside for that render.
