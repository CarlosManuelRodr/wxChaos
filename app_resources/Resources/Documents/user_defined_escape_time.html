What to try first

Begin with a known rule such as z = z^2 + c. Confirm that you recognize the behavior before changing it.

Change one ingredient at a time: the power, a sine term, a conjugate, or a small constant.

Use the orbit display to debug your intuition. If the image surprises you, the orbit usually explains why.

Mathematical notes

What escape-time means

An escape-time renderer repeats a recurrence until either the orbit passes a bailout radius or the iteration limit is reached. Pixels that escape are colored by when or how they escaped; pixels that do not escape are treated as inside for that render.

Notas matemáticas

Qué significa tiempo de escape

Un renderizador de tiempo de escape repite una recurrencia hasta que la órbita supera un radio de escape o se alcanza el límite de iteraciones. Los píxeles que escapan se colorean según cuándo o cómo escaparon; los que no escapan se consideran interiores para ese renderizado.
