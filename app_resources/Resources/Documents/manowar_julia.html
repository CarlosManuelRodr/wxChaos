Manowar Julia

A fixed-parameter Julia view of the Manowar recurrence.

Formula

A Manowar-style recurrence with a fixed complex parameter \(k\).

Background

As with quadratic Julia sets, fixing the parameter turns the parameter-plane family into a dynamical-plane view.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Smooth coloring and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
