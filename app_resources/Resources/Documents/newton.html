Newton Fractal

Basins of attraction for Newton-Raphson root finding.

Formula

\(z_{n+1}=z_n-\frac{f(z_n)}{f'(z_n)}\), with \(f(z)=z^3-1\).

Background

Newton's method is a numerical method for finding roots. Coloring each starting point by the root it reaches reveals the fractal boundaries between basins of attraction. The roots themselves are simple, but the boundary between “which root wins” can be infinitely complicated.
