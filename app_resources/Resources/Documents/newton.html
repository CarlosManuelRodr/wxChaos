Newton Fractal

Basins of attraction for Newton-Raphson root finding.

Formula

\(z_{n+1}=z_n-\frac{f(z_n)}{f'(z_n)}\), with \(f(z)=z^3-1\).

Background

Newton's method is a numerical method for finding roots. Coloring each starting point by the root it reaches reveals the fractal boundaries between basins of attraction.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Orbit trap mode for convergence rendering.
  • Algorithm: convergence test.
