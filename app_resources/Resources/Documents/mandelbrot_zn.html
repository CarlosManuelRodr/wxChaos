Mandelbrot ZN

A power-family generalization of the Mandelbrot set.

Formula

\(z_{0}=0,\quad z_{n+1}=z_n^p+c\). The exponent is controlled by the fractal options.

Background

Changing the exponent keeps the Mandelbrot idea but changes the symmetry and number of major bulbs. These multibrot sets are a common way to study how polynomial degree shapes complex dynamics.

wxChaos capabilities

  • High precision rendering.
  • Orbit display, smooth coloring, and orbit traps.
  • Algorithms: escape time, Gaussian integer, escape angle, triangle inequality.
