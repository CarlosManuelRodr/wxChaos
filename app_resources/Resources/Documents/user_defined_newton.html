What to try first

Start with z^3 - 1. Three roots usually produce three clear basins.

Try changing the degree or adding a small term. More roots can make the basin borders more intricate.

Use the orbit display near a boundary to see Newton's method hesitate, jump, and finally settle.

Mathematical notes

Newton iteration for your function

For a function \(f\), Newton's method uses \(z_{n+1}=z_n-f(z_n)/f'(z_n)\). The derivative points toward a local linear estimate of a root. When the estimate is good, convergence can be fast; near basin boundaries or derivative trouble, the path can become chaotic.
