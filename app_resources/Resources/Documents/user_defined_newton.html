User Defined Newton-Raphson

A Newton fractal generated from a user-supplied function.

Formula

\(z_{n+1}=z_n-\frac{f(z_n)}{f'(z_n)}\), where \(f\) is supplied by the user.

Background

The symbolic compiler derives the derivative, making this mode useful for exploring root basins of custom functions.

wxChaos capabilities

  • Symbolic derivative generation through SymEngine.
  • Formula evaluation through muParserX.
  • Algorithm: convergence test.
